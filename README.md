# Mobile_Sales_Analysis

## Project Overview

Mobile businesses generate large volumes of transactional data across different brands, mobile models, cities, payment methods, and time periods. Turning this data into clear business insights can help organizations understand sales performance, customer preferences, and high-performing products.

This project presents an interactive **Mobile Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze mobile sales performance and provide a clear view of key business KPIs, sales trends, product performance, payment preferences, customer ratings, and geographic distribution.

The dashboard brings multiple dimensions of mobile sales data into a single business intelligence view, helping stakeholders monitor performance and identify areas of opportunity.

---

## Business Problem

Mobile retailers need to answer important business questions such as:

- Which mobile brands and models generate the highest sales?
- How does sales quantity change throughout the year?
- Which cities contribute significantly to total sales?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- How are customer ratings distributed?
- Which days of the week generate the highest sales?
- Which products and brands should receive greater business attention?

The objective of this project was to transform mobile sales data into an interactive dashboard that supports faster and more informed business decision-making.

## Dashboard Features

The Mobile Sales Dashboard includes the following components:

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides high-level KPIs for monitoring:

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Total Transactions
- Additional sales performance metric

The dashboard snapshot shows approximately **69M in total sales**, **2K in total quantity**, and **324 transactions**.

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically explore the dashboard using:

- Mobile Model
- Payment Method
- Brand
- Month

These filters make it possible to analyze specific products, brands, payment methods, or time periods.

### Sales by City

A geographic visualization displays total sales across major cities, helping identify the locations contributing to mobile sales performance.

### Quantity by Month

A monthly trend chart tracks total quantity from January through December and helps identify changes in sales volume throughout the year.

### Ratings Analysis

The **Ratings by Rating Status** visualization compares customer rating categories such as:

- Good
- Average
- Poor

This helps understand the overall distribution of customer ratings.

### Payment Method Analysis

The dashboard includes a transaction distribution chart covering:

- Debit Card
- Cash
- UPI
- Credit Card

The visualization helps identify customer payment preferences.

### Brand Performance

A detailed table compares brands using:

- Brand
- Total Sales
- Transactions

The dashboard snapshot includes brands such as Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, and Vivo.

### Mobile Model Performance

A bar chart highlights the highest-performing mobile models based on total sales.

Examples visible in the dashboard include:

- Vivo S1
- Vivo V20
- iPhone 11

### Sales by Day

The dashboard analyzes total sales across days of the week to identify periods with relatively higher and lower sales activity.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard view:

- Total sales are approximately **69M**, with **324 transactions** displayed in the KPI section.
- **Vivo** is one of the strongest-performing brands in the displayed brand comparison, with approximately **16.7M** in total sales.
- Among the displayed mobile models, **Vivo S1** generates approximately **6.6M** in sales, followed by **Vivo V20** at approximately **6.4M**.
- Monthly quantity varies throughout the year, with **March and July** showing particularly strong quantities in the displayed trend.
- The payment method chart shows transactions distributed across Debit Card, Cash, UPI, and Credit Card, indicating the importance of supporting multiple payment options.
- The dashboard shows a larger share of customers in the **Good** rating category compared with Average and Poor.
- Sales vary by day of the week, with **Thursday and Saturday** appearing among the stronger-performing days in the displayed chart.
- City-level visualization provides a geographic view of sales performance and can be used to identify important markets.
